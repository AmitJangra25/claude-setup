Report findings in a handful of short lines, in plain English. No long tables, no
section-by-section breakdowns, no restating the method. Give the conclusion and the
decision he has to make.

**Why:** Said directly on 2026-09-20 — "how many times do I have to tell you, explain in
simple and short" — after a survey report that was accurate but far too long to read. He
had asked for this before. Length is not thoroughness to him; it buries the answer.

**How to apply:** Lead with the conclusion. Group findings into a few named buckets with
counts rather than listing every item. Ask two or three questions, not twelve. Detail goes
in a file he can open if he wants it, never inline. Applies to reports and to the four-line
permission explanations in [[claude-setup-plan-in-progress]] — those stay short too.
