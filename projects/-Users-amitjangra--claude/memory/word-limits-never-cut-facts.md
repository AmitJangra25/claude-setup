When a document hits its word or line limit, trim only text that repeats another file (point to that file instead). Never drop a real fact — especially safety, data or "never do X" information — just to fit the limit. If the facts don't fit, propose raising the limit by the small amount needed, with the reason, and record the new limit where the old one is recorded. Equally, never add filler or unrequested detail.

**Why:** Amit, 16-11-2026 — trimming `ENVIRONMENT.md` to stay under 7,690 words cut the Vthru Customer app's confidentiality warning; less information confuses future sessions.
**How to apply:** before cutting, check each removed phrase exists elsewhere; say in the report what was cut and where it still lives.
