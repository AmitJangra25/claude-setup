Write rules, guides, plans and replies in simple, everyday human English — the way a person explains something to a colleague, not stiff "AI English". Keep it short where that helps, but never force it short just because Amit said "short", and never cut facts to get there.

**Why:** Amit, 24-11-2026 — clear simple writing is easier for both humans and AI to follow, so work moves faster and uses fewer tokens. He once said "Indian English"; he meant only simple human English — ignore the label.
**How to apply:** plain words, short sentences, a small example where a rule could be misread. Related: [[word-limits-never-cut-facts]].
