Agreement during discussion, including dictated speech, is not approval. Change files only after Amit gives an explicit yes to the approval question, such as "yes, add them" or "go ahead."

**Why:** 26-11-2026 — Claude asked "can I add these 5 changes?", Amit then discussed a side point by voice ("Yes, I completely agree…"), and Claude made the 5 edits. Amit had not approved them; they were undone.

**How to apply:** when an approval question is open and Amit's next message discusses something else, answer the discussion and ask the approval question again. See [[preconditions-are-hard-stops]] and [[stay-on-the-asked-task]].
